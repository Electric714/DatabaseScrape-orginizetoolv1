A SIMPLE WORKFLOW

Make yourself at home.

  1. Add a website.

    Give a permitted public directory a name and paste its address.

  2. Start a scan.

    Follow the activity console. A partial scan means a limit or error needs attention.

  3. Explore the records.

    Search, filter by source, browse pages, or export your results.

  4. Get help with context.

    Capture a snapshot, then export the diagnostic ZIP. Activity is saved even after a restart.

Keep the launcher window open while using the app. Press Ctrl+C there when you’re finished.
